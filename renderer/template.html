Cumulative charts · counting and ranking

Each view ranks up to 50 artists or songs by its selected measure over the full archive, after applying the duration and known-family filters. Listens counts qualifying export events (default at least 30 seconds), not completed songs or official Spotify-counted streams. Recorded minutes adds the actual milliseconds played, including partial plays that pass the duration filter. Select All events to include short and zero-duration rows. Lines are exact event-time steps, beginning at zero at the archive origin; simultaneous timestamps are summed. Zooming preserves all prior qualifying events.

Artists use the exact exported name on each original event, without guessing identity or full credits. Songs retain distinct Spotify track IDs, including separate editions; album and ID are available in the list and inspector. Search narrows the chooser only and never changes the top-50 universe or hides checked lines. Rankings do not change with date zoom. Each artist/song and metric/filter combination remembers its selection during this page session. The secondary monthly bars show new listening in that month for the selected lines; they are not cumulative. The shaded tail after the export endpoint is unavailable history, not evidence of no later listening.

How to read this · methods, coverage, and limitations

01 / Sound spectrum

The vertical coordinate is the first principal component (PCA) of eight standardized audio features: energy, danceability, valence, acousticness, instrumentalness, speechiness, loudness, and tempo. Values are clipped to ±4 standard deviations before fitting. Tracks are fitted equally, not weighted by how often you played them. The axis points toward increasing acousticness; its ends are described from the actual feature loadings. This is a sonic similarity axis, not a genre label or a mood diagnosis. A one-dimensional projection necessarily loses information.

02 / Cluster lanes

K-means uses the same eight-feature space, a fixed seed (42), and 15 initializations. Compare 6, 10, or 14 clusters. Cluster lanes are ordered along the PCA axis; the ordering is not a ranking. Within each lane, a small deterministic track offset separates overlaps; that offset has no independent meaning. Descriptive names use the two strongest standardized centroid contrasts, not inferred genres. Representative tracks and artists are ranked by recorded playback in the full archive, not just the selected window.

Colors always identify the current audio cluster in the two sound views. They do not imply that a cluster is one genre. Clusters are exploratory choices, not a scientifically unique partition.

03 / Genre map & data boundaries

Only existing Last.fm broad track-tag families determine genre positions. Families are ordered by average-linkage clustering of Jaccard co-tag distance, with optimal leaf ordering. Multi-family tracks sit at the mean of their family coordinates, so different tag combinations can overlap. Hover a family to highlight every matching track, wherever it sits. A dot's color uses its first family on the ordered axis only; no exclusive classification is implied. Unknown tracks stay in a separate gray lane.

Other available stats.fm track-release, album-release, and credited-artist labels are shown separately in track tooltips. Artist tags are never silently substituted for track genres. No labels are invented for missing data.

Exact duplicates are excluded; short and zero-duration events are retained in the file and optionally filtered. Audio-track records only: podcasts, video, and audiobooks are outside this chart. UTC stream-end timestamps determine dates. Summed recorded durations are not guaranteed non-overlapping wall-clock time. This export is not live; an empty tail is not evidence of no listening. Provider estimates and cached tags may be imperfect.
