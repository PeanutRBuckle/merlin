Import your history

Select all of your Spotify Extended Streaming History JSON files. Merlin combines them here, on your device.

Don’t have your history yet?

  1. Open Spotify privacy settings ↗.
  2. Request “Extended streaming history.” Spotify prepares the download separately; it may take several weeks.
  3. Unzip the download, then select the audio history JSON files. You can also reopen a previously exported Merlin file.

No Spotify login is needed here. Source IP addresses are discarded from prepared data. Audio-track views exclude podcasts, audiobooks, and video records.
